# Code of conduct

Interleave welcomes technical discussion, bug reports and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and specific. Critique code, evidence and ideas without attacking the person who shared them.
- Make room for questions and corrections. Assume mistakes can be fixed, and acknowledge evidence that changes your view.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project. Give maintainers and contributors enough context to understand a concern.
- Respect boundaries and requests to stop an unwanted interaction.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning remarks, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission. Do not expose credentials, private SQL, production data or other sensitive material in project spaces.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to Pavan Gupta at [pavan.gupta.352@gmail.com](mailto:pavan.gupta.352@gmail.com). Include the relevant links or context and describe what happened. Avoid copying private information that is not needed to assess the report.

Use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security vulnerabilities. Do not open a public issue for a security report.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock a discussion, reject a contribution, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or ban someone from project spaces. The response will consider the conduct, its impact, whether it is repeated and what is needed to keep project work safe and productive.

This code applies to participation in the Interleave repository and other project-managed spaces.
